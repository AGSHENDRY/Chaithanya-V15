CHAITHANYA - ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES

1. On the VPS, inside the project folder, create a file named exactly:
   .env

2. Copy the contents of .env.example into .env and fill in your real values.

3. NEVER upload .env to GitHub. It contains secrets such as the MongoDB password.

Example:
NODE_ENV=production
PORT=3000
HOST=127.0.0.1
MONGODB_URI=mongodb+srv://USERNAME:PASSWORD@CLUSTER.mongodb.net/
MONGODB_DB=chaithanya
MONGO_MAX_POOL_SIZE=10
SMTP_HOST=
SMTP_PORT=587
SMTP_USER=
SMTP_PASS=
MAIL_FROM=
CORS_ORIGIN=https://chaithanya.shop

After changing .env, restart the app:
  pm2 restart chaithanya

Check:
  curl http://127.0.0.1:3000/api/health
